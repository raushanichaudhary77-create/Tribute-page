About Him

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes.

He was known for his simplicity, dedication, humility and love for students. His life continues to inspire millions of young people to dream big and work hard.

His Admirable Qualities

🌟 Inspiration

He inspired young people to dream big and believe in their abilities.

📚 Knowledge

He believed that education and knowledge were powerful tools for building a better future.

💙 Humility

Despite his achievements, he remained humble, simple and connected with people.

Important Achievements

  • Played an important role in India's missile development.
  • Contributed to India's space and defence programmes.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.
  • Inspired millions of students through his books and speeches.
